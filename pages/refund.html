Refund and cancellation policy

Last updated 14 September 2026.

Donations are generally not refundable

A donation is a voluntary gift and is committed to charitable work as soon as it is received, so as a rule we do not refund donations.

When we will refund

  • Duplicate or repeated payment. If the same donation was charged more than once, we refund the extra payments in full.
  • Wrong amount entered. If you meant to give a different amount and tell us within seven days, we will refund the difference or the whole amount.
  • Unauthorised payment. If a payment was made without your authority, tell us and your bank at once and we will refund it once the transaction is confirmed as unauthorised.
  • Technical failure. If money left your account but the donation did not register, we refund it.

How to ask

Email info@caspianfoundation.in or call +91 89784 54547 with the payment reference, the date and the amount. We acknowledge within three working days.

How refunds are made

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay. Banks usually credit the money within five to ten working days of us processing it. If an 80G receipt was already issued for that donation, it is cancelled and you must not claim a deduction against it.

Event registrations

Where a Foundation event has a paid pass, the refund terms for that event are published on that event's own page and apply instead of this policy.
