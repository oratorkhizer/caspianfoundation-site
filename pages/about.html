About

A small trust with a narrow job, done properly.

Caspian Healthcare Foundation was created in 2023 to do the part of diabetes and obesity care that nobody pays for: finding the disease early in people who cannot afford to look for it, and teaching, in public and to doctors, what to do next.

Our purpose

Mission

To empower communities through accessible medical services, health education and charitable support, without discrimination of caste, creed, religion or sex.

That sentence is taken from our trust deed, and it decides the work. We do not run a parallel clinic for the well off. We go where the testing has not reached, we publish what things cost, and we say plainly when we do not know something.

What the deed commits us to

  • Establishing free clinics, diagnostic services and medical camps
  • Providing scholarships and educational grants
  • Offering subsidised and free health screening and treatment
  • Supporting relief for the poor, the elderly and the differently abled
  • Advancing research, training and public health education

How we govern ourselves

Governance

Board and meetings

The board meets quarterly. The Managing Trustee is authorised under the deed to sign documents and agreements on behalf of the board. Bank operation requires the authorisation set out in the deed.

Accounts and audit

The Foundation's accounts are audited annually and its returns are filed with the Income Tax Department. Activities that are ancillary to the charitable objects are accounted for separately.

Use of income

Income is applied solely to the objects of the trust. No part of it is distributed to trustees, and no trustee receives remuneration for holding office.

Independence from industry

Companies may fund a programme. No funder decides what our doctors teach, which treatment is recommended, or what a screening report says. Where a programme has a sponsor, we name the sponsor.

A common question

How the Foundation relates to Caspian Healthcare

Caspian Healthcare Foundation is a charitable trust and is a separate legal entity from the Caspian Healthcare clinic and its group company. They share a founder and, at times, premises and clinical volunteers.

Donations to the Foundation fund charitable work only. They do not pay for anybody's treatment at commercial rates, and they are not income of the clinic.
