Donating from outside India

This page takes Indian cards, UPI and netbanking. It does not take foreign cards, because Razorpay does not offer international payments to charitable organisations.

There is a second reason, and it is the more important one. Under the Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Act, a trust may accept money from a foreign source only if it holds FCRA registration. The Foundation does not hold one, so we cannot accept a donation from a foreign citizen, including a person of Indian origin who has taken another citizenship.

An NRI who still holds an Indian passport is not a foreign source. A donation from your own savings is welcome, and you can enter your overseas mobile number with its country code above.

If you want to support the work and none of this fits your situation, write to info@caspianfoundation.in and we will tell you honestly what we can and cannot accept.
