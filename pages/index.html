Why this work exists

India has more people living with diabetes than any country has ever had.

The ICMR-INDIAB national survey put the figure at 101 million adults with diabetes and another 136 million with prediabetes, alongside 254 million with generalised obesity. Most of them have never had a single test. In the lanes around our clinic, a blood sugar reading is not a routine number, it is a piece of news that arrives ten years late, usually with an ulcer, a failing kidney or a first heart attack attached to it.

A full screening costs about one thousand rupees to deliver. That is the entire gap between someone finding out early and someone finding out too late.

Find it early

Screening camps

Blood glucose, blood pressure, height, weight and waist, done free, with the result explained face to face by a doctor rather than handed over as a printout.

Explain it plainly

Public education

Talks and clinics in everyday language for people who have been told to control their sugar and never told how. No product placement, no sponsor on the slide.

Make it last

Training doctors

The Foundation hosts a structured certificate course in obesity medicine for practising physicians, so good care outlives any one camp or any one clinic.

What we run

Four programmes, one thread

Everything the Foundation does follows the same line: find the disease early, explain it honestly, and keep treating it after the camp has packed up.

Annual · 14 November 2026

Caspian Diabesity Expo

Our flagship public event, run with the Telangana chapter of RSSDI. Free entry, free multi-parameter screening including blood glucose, lipid profile, liver and kidney function, TSH, vitamin D, ECG, retinal and neuropathy checks, FibroScan and body measurements, plus expert sessions and a free meal.

diabesityexpo.com

Regular

Caspian Diabetes Talks

Community lectures on the complications people actually fear, with free blood glucose, blood pressure and BMI checks on the spot and an open question and answer session with the doctor afterwards.

Peer support

OWN, the Obesity Warriors Network

A moderated support group for patients on GLP-1 treatment, with guidance from Dr Khizer Junaidy, peer encouragement and practical content between appointments. Open to Caspian Healthcare patients on that treatment.

For doctors

CASPIAN Certificate in Obesity Medicine

A twelve-module certificate course for practising physicians, hosted by the Foundation, with a textbook, case-based teaching and assessments. Independent of pharmaceutical sponsorship.

Where your money goes

One thousand rupees is one whole person, screened.

We cost our work at what it actually takes to deliver, not at what sounds impressive in a report. A full screening, the consumables, the staff time and the doctor who sits down and explains the result, comes to roughly one thousand rupees a head.

  • Rs 1,000 screens one person completely, result explained
  • Rs 3,000 covers a family
  • Rs 10,000 puts a screening bench into a community camp
  • Rs 25,000 underwrites a full neighbourhood talk with screening

Trustees hold honorary office and draw no salary from the trust. Income is applied solely to the objects of the trust.
