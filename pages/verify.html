Certificates

Verify a certificate.

Scan the QR code on the certificate, or type the certificate number and the verification code printed at the bottom of it. We show what it was issued for, so you can match it with the paper.

Both are printed at the bottom of the certificate. The code is 8 letters and numbers; capitals and the dash do not matter.

About these certificates

The Foundation runs training programmes with the departments of Caspian Healthcare, including the internship and classes in psychiatry for psychology students. Every certificate it issues is recorded here with its own number and private code. A certificate that does not match, or that the Foundation has withdrawn, is shown as such.

If a certificate does not verify and you think it should, write to us with the certificate number. The Foundation will never ask you to send the verification code to anyone.
