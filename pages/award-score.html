Jury scoring

How to score

  • Score each entry against its rubric. The points for each criterion are shown beside it. The total is out of 100.
  • Write three to five lines of feedback. The applicant receives it, so make it specific and kind.
  • You can save a draft and come back. Press Submit when you are done. You can still change a submitted score until the round closes.
  • If you know the applicant personally or work with their institution, press I have a conflict. The secretariat will give the entry to another juror.
  • Do not share entries or scores with anyone. Round 1 closes on 5 November, finalist scoring on 9 November.
