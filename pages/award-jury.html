Help us judge the work.

The jury is chosen through open applications, so the jury itself is selected on merit. The role is honorary, online, and takes about six to eight hours between 27 October and 9 November.

Who can apply

Registered medical practitioners with at least ten years of practice after postgraduation in diabetology, endocrinology, internal medicine or a related field. Senior academics in public health, epidemiology or biostatistics with diabetes or obesity work are also welcome.

How jurors are chosen

Standing in the field, a spread of expertise (clinical, academic and data), a spread across regions of India, and no conflict with likely entrants. The convenor may also invite jurors directly if needed.

What jurors receive

A certificate of appreciation from the Foundation, your name on the published jury list, and acknowledgement at the felicitation on World Diabetes Day. Jurors may not enter the award in the same year.
