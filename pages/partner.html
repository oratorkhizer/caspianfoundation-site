Partnership

Fund a programme, not a banner.

We work with companies, hospitals, professional societies and community organisations. What a partner buys is a defined piece of work, delivered and reported. What a partner does not buy is influence over what our doctors say.

Ways to work together

Four kinds of partnership

Sponsor a screening drive

You fund the screening of a defined number of people in a defined area. We deliver it, and you receive the counts, the abnormal detection rate and the referral outcomes. Costed at what screening actually takes, about one thousand rupees a person.

Support the Diabesity Expo

Our annual public day. Partners support screening stations, the public programme or the venue, and are recognised by name. Exhibition space is available separately. The 2026 edition is on 14 November.

Academic and CME collaboration

Symposiums, workshops, clinical updates, skill sessions, conferences and webinars, in person or blended. Faculty and content are set by the Foundation. Support is acknowledged openly and separately from the science.

Host a camp for your workforce or community

Residents' associations, workplaces, schools and congregations can host a talk with screening. You provide the room and the audience, we bring the team and the testing.

The terms, stated up front

What a partner gets, and what a partner does not

You get

  • A written scope: what will be delivered, where, to how many people
  • An 80G receipt under URN AADTC2568A24HY01
  • A report after the event with real counts, not estimates
  • Named recognition, on the terms agreed in writing

You do not get

  • Any say over clinical content, faculty or treatment recommendations
  • Access to the personal details of the people screened
  • Promotion of a named product by our doctors from our platform

This is not a formality. Independence is the reason our programmes are worth sponsoring in the first place.
